+ THE HYBRID MAGIC SYSTEM
ARCHIVES
Seven classical elements: Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Light, Dark, and Void. Choose your primary Archive and
specialize inits techniques, or struggle through cross-Archive learning for versatility.
GRIMOIRES
Your magical catalyst. It can be anything Ancient Bloodline grimoires carry centuries of power and high
resonance. First-gen mages receive standard academy issue. Quality determines your ceiling.
THEMA
A personal philosophical concept that shapes how you manifest magic. Cannot be chosen—only.
discovered. Your Thema reveals your true self and defines your signature techniques. Can be refined,
evolved, or even refted.
FREE-FORM SPELLCASTING
No rigid spell slots. Learn any spell you can access through curriculum, research,
‘mentorship, or discovery. Primary Archive spells are efficient; cross-Archive magic
requires more effort. Your grimoire resonance (0-100%) determines power output and
success rate.
